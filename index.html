Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 18, 2026  |  Developer: Anshil Technical (Pooja Maurya)

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Anshil Technical (Pooja Maurya) handles information when you use the Arrow Puzzle Android mobile application (package com.aistudio.arrowpuzzle.pmkqvx).

Arrow Puzzle is designed as a privacy-first offline puzzle experience with ad support. We do not require user registration, accounts, or sign-in, and we do not operate developer servers that store your personal information.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

All gameplay progress and audio/accessibility preferences are saved locally on your device inside private application storage:

  • Unlocked levels, completed levels, stars earned, and Daily Challenge streaks.
  • Background music toggle and volume, sound effects toggle and volume, victory music preference, and haptic feedback settings.

This local data stays on your device and can be erased at any time using Reset Game Progress in the app or by uninstalling Arrow Puzzle.

3. Advertising & Third-Party Services (Google AdMob)

Arrow Puzzle uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to display interstitial ads strictly after every 5 completed levels when tapping Next Level (never interrupting active puzzle moves).

Google and its advertising partners may collect and process technical device signals to serve and measure ads, including:

  • Device Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID).
  • Approximate network/IP-based country or region, device model, OS version, and ad interaction metrics.
  • Basic SDK crash and performance diagnostics.

For full details on how Google handles advertising data, please review the Google Privacy Policy and Google Advertising Technologies.

4. Device Permissions Used

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used solely to request and load Google AdMob ads when a network connection is available.
  • VIBRATE: Used only for optional tactile haptic feedback during button and arrow taps (controllable in Settings).

Arrow Puzzle does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, precise GPS location, call logs, or external photo/file storage.

5. Your Privacy Controls & Choices

  • Ad Personalization: You can opt out of personalized ads or reset/delete your Advertising ID anytime in Android Settings → Privacy / Security & Privacy → Ads.
  • Reset Local Progress: Use Reset Game Progress on the Home or Profile screen to clear saved levels and stars immediately.
  • Audio & Haptics: Independently toggle background music, sound effects, victory music, and vibration in Settings.

6. Children's Privacy

Arrow Puzzle does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age threshold in your jurisdiction). If you believe personal data has been provided inadvertently, please contact us so we can address it promptly.

7. Policy Updates & Contact Information

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect app updates or regulatory changes. Updates will appear with a revised effective date in this document and inside the application.
